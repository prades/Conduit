What this does. Console music is not stored as audio — it is sequence data driving a sound chip, plus a bank of instrument samples. Those samples are real audio. This scans a ROM for BRR blocks, the SNES sample format, decodes them exactly, and hands you the results as playable instruments. Raise MIN LENGTH if you are seeing noise; lower it to catch short percussion.
Where it works. Best on SNES ROMs, since BRR is theirs. Other systems store samples in formats this does not decode, so expect few or no hits — the scanner only reports runs that are structurally valid, rather than guessing.
Your files stay here. Nothing is uploaded and no ROM is included; load one you dumped yourself. Audio you extract is someone's copyrighted work, same as any other sample — fine for your own use, worth clearing before you release anything built on it.
