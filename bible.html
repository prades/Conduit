The game 01

Conduit is a browser game in a dark, ruined research facility, seen from above at an angle (isometric) and played mostly on an iPad, by touch; it plays on a desktop with the mouse and keys, and on a phone with its own layout. It is written in plain JavaScript on a 2D canvas, with no engine and nothing to install: game.html is the game, and the home page's Conduit card opens it.

How a game goes

The game's own guide (the ? in the dock: game.html, "HOW A GAME GOES"), word for word.

  1. Find supplies in the dark. The facility opens in a blackout; your monitor lamp is your light. Supply cases glow: walk over one to take it. Press I for your pack.
  2. Restore a relay. Each relay powers a few rooms. Walk up to a broken one with the relay kit and two power cells (a Technomancer needs one cell and no kit) and it is repaired: its rooms light up.
  3. Gather a squad. Three followers walk with you. Hatch the eggs on the pads around the facility, or buy a recruit at the Crystal in the ROSTER for 30 ◆. The rest hold rooms.
  4. Hunt the arena. Tap a creature and your gun locks on; click or hold the round dial to fire. A kill pays shards on the spot and drops DNA for the Clone Bay and scrap for the Armory.
  5. Level, evolve, clone. Followers level from kills and evolve at level 10. Spend DNA and shards in the CLONE BAY on a creature of your own.
  6. Defend the Crystal. An alarm sends the creatures hunting, and they go for the Crystal. HOLD followers near it. If the Crystal falls, the game is over.
