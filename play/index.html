What this is. A front end around EmulatorJS, which runs libretro emulator cores compiled to WebAssembly. The emulators are mature open-source projects; this page only loads your file into one. Cores download from the EmulatorJS CDN on first use.
Controls. The emulator supplies its own on-screen controls, wired straight into the core. ⚙ TOUCH SETUP offers an alternative custom overlay, off by default since it covers theirs. A paired Bluetooth controller works either way and beats glass for precise steering.
Which systems actually work. SNES, Game Boy, GBA, Mega Drive and NES run flawlessly — they were built for hardware thousands of times slower than your tablet. PlayStation is comfortable too; prefer a compressed .chd over a raw .bin. N64 is the one to avoid — it is the hardest system to emulate and crashes when levels load, because a browser tab runs out of memory.
No games or BIOS files are included. Load dumps of discs and cartridges you own.
