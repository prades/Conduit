Conduit balance

Written against build 2026-11-13w. Made by node tools/balance.js from the code, so it cannot drift; nothing here is history (that is docs/CHANGELOG.md and the Bible). Tap a column heading to sort.

Every form below is level 20 with the same base stats, so the numbers compare forms and not luck. Power is its ATK; the Mage's shot reads SP.ATK. A primary attack carries its status on 25% of its hits; a blinded creature misses 50% of its hits.

2. The score: points a second, and the role bands

Everything a form does is counted in one currency. 1 damage = 1 point. 1 HP healed, blocked, or added to an ally's hit = 1 point. Holding an enemy off is worth the damage it would have dealt: one enemy deals about 7.5 a second here, so a second of an enemy stunned or lured away is worth all of that, blinded 50% of it, poisoned 40% of it, slowed 25% of it (the slow's share is provisional).

A form's score is its best mode. Each role's target is the middle score of its forms today, and its band is 25% either way: Brawler 37.7 to 62.9 (target 50.3); Mage 15.8 to 26.3 (target 21); Technician 14.1 to 23.5 (target 18.8). The targets are yours to set; these only describe the game as it is.
