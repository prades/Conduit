Conduit in one line: the facility is dark and its power is down. Find supplies, repair the main power junction to bring every relay back online, gather a squad of followers, and keep the Crystal alive against what comes out of the arena.
HOW A GAME GOES
1
Find supplies in the dark
The facility opens in a blackout; your monitor lamp is your light. Supply cases glow: walk over one to take it. Press I for your pack.
2
Repair the junction
The main power junction in the Server Room is missing its power module. Side Quest flies up to the catwalks for it; install it at the junction and every relay comes on.
3
Gather a squad
Three followers walk with you. Hatch the eggs on the pads around the facility, or buy a recruit at the Crystal in the ROSTER for 30 ◆. The rest hold rooms.
4
Hunt the arena
Tap a creature and your gun locks on; click or hold the round dial to fire. A kill pays shards on the spot and drops DNA for the Clone Bay and scrap for the Armory.
5
Level, evolve, clone
Followers level from kills and evolve at level 10. Spend DNA and shards in the CLONE BAY on a creature of your own.
6
Defend the Crystal
An alarm sends the creatures hunting, and they go for the Crystal. HOLD followers near it. If the Crystal falls, the game is over.
THE THREE MOVES
TAP THE FLOOR
Walk there, through the doors. Tap a station or a case to use it or take it.
TAP A CREATURE
Lock your gun on it; the round dial fires. Tap the enemy card to send the squad at it.
LONG-PRESS A FOLLOWER
Its card: numbers, INDEX and DISMISS. Long-press a creature for INFO or ATTACK.
In detail — the tutorial and the classes
THE OBJECTIVE PLATE

      
The plate at the top right (on a phone, a small envelope that wears a ! when there is something new; tap it to unfold it) says what to do next: find supplies, find the broken junction, get its power module, repair the junction, recruit a follower, post two followers near the Crystal, fire your gun, defend the Crystal against a first assault, evolve a follower. The game is saved every few seconds; SETTINGS → NEW GAME throws the save away.

      
THE CLASSES

      
When the tutorial is done you choose a class (it asks you to confirm), its supply cases arrive, and the class's own stage follows (the Crystal gives each step in the dialogue box; QUESTS lists them): its gear at its station, then its loop (the Gunslinger: the cameras, a flare, a hunt; the Technomancer: punches, arcs; the Mutator: a clone, an upgrade, the lash, a serum). Any class may carry another class's gear, but only the right class may use it.

      
Switch class any time from your pack (SWITCH CLASS, then confirm). Each class keeps its own progress — its perks and rank, its objectives, the weapon it last held, and the Mutator's clone, which waits for it — and picks up where it left off when you switch back. Its kit comes the first time only. It all lasts until the Crystal is destroyed.

      
GUNSLINGERWeapons, ammunition and tactical gear. Flares to light the dark; the Armory's machine gun; CCTV sweeps from the Security Room's console that mark every enemy in the facility for a while.

      
TECHNOMANCERElectrical infrastructure. Fights with the arc gauntlet: a shock punch up close stores a charge, and out of reach a charge throws an electric arc that jumps to the creatures beside it and cuts short a bite being wound up. A powered room charges the gauntlet, and every relay online makes the arc stronger. A tuned lamp from the console in Teleport Room 1; the spider mech built in the Warehouse's mech bay.

      
MUTATORBiological samples. Fights up close with the tentacle lash, beside one clone it grows at the Clone Bay and upgrades with DNA (a fallen clone stays fallen: salvage its body, or have an Ice Mage preserve it and carry the capsule back to the Crystal); the healing serum from the Experiment Lab's hood splashes a pool that heals allies. A mutagen sample raises the nearest follower a level (two once the lab's sequencer has been used); the lab coat, put on by walking through the lab.

      
The Armory's terminal (once the Armory relay is online) turns scrap metal from the arena into ammunition, equips a weapon you have, and upgrades or assembles one: the service gun everyone starts with, the Gunslinger's machine gun, and a laser rifle that runs on fuel cells.

      
THE SEWERS

      
Down the culvert behind the arena's north tank. The water-quality reading as you arrive begins PURIFY THE SEWERS: three samples from the toxic ants down there and the water sample on the bank across the river at its west end, cut off by the water and a rock fall (three ways, each alone: an ice technician freezes a bridge over the river; a fire technician drills the rock fall from the south ledge; or an electric technician sets a launcher up on the north ledge and you ride it over (tap its tag twice), take the vial and crawl back through the drain pipe, whose grate then stays open as a shortcut); the Experiment Lab's analysis bench takes them and sells the purification module for 300 ◆; the module opens the boss gate and is installed at the console by the sump (about ten seconds), which wakes the Toxic Ant Queen. She sits rooted beside the console, throws toxic bombs (move: they land where you stood), spits when you are close, and calls toxic nymphs, four at a time, whose bites poison you for a moment; the install holds while she is at the console. Asleep, stunned, frozen or glitched she lets go of it; at half her health she goes frantic and roams: lure her off and work. Her bites do not stop the install; walking away does. Or kill her. Once the samples are in and ten of the ants are dead, Side Quest, the void walker on the maintenance platform off the north ledge, sells a sleeping potion (100 ◆; half once it has put her to sleep): EQUIP it in the pack and the POTION pill, or T, throws it; it sleeps everything near where it lands for five seconds, your followers too, never you (the Experiment Lab's bench copies it at a quarter strength, enemies only). When the module has run, the water runs clean, the brood thins out, and a living queen calms and keeps her post. From then on Side Quest also sells the Sewers' three toxic Player cards, BLIGHT SHOOTER, SMOG ENGINEER and PLAGUE DOCTOR, on the buttons under his line (1000 ◆ each, kept for good, and yours to wear at once; not on a phone yet).

      

    


    
    


Everything is a tap: the floor to walk, a creature to aim, a follower's card for its orders. The long-press ring is for a creature (INFO, ATTACK) and for your followers (their card).
ON THE FLOOR

      
· Tap the floor → walk there (a path is found through the doors). Tap a glowing supply case → walk over and take it. Tap a station (a relay, a locker, a console) or stand at it and press E → use it; a plate says what it does and what it costs

      
◎ Tap a creature → your gun locks on to it (near one it locks by itself: the nearest you can see within 9 tiles). The round dial with its picture appears: click it for a shot, hold it to keep firing. Tap another creature to lock that one; the ammunition chip under the dial puts the gun away

      
⌀ Long-press a creature → its ring: INFO (its numbers, and a way into the canvas index of everything) or ATTACK. Long-press one of your followers → its card, the same as tapping it on the squad bar

      
✦✦ Double-tap a follower → fire its ultimate (when the bar under its health is full)


      
THE SQUAD BAR AND THE ORDERS

      
Your squad is three followers: their small cards stand in a strip along the bottom, right of the orders (on a phone, across the top of the dock); the tab at its end slides them away and back. A tap on a card picks that member — the orders go to it alone and its card rolls up at the bottom, with its energy, its attack and its cards (a tap anywhere else puts that card away, and the member stays picked) — and ALL lets it go. An open slot opens the roster to pick who fills it.

      
FALLEN A follower that falls comes back at the Crystal, and the Crystal pays 5–10% of its power (more for one with more energy cards). It waits there: walk back to the Crystal Chamber and the ones from your squad rejoin it. COME cannot reach it

      
ROSTER The long card at the bottom left, with ALL, HOLD and COME standing behind it: a tap opens the roster. While a creature's card is up it stands over that card

      
RUN On a creature's card (RETREAT on a phone): the picked followers break off and fall back to you for six seconds

      
HOLD Each one holds the room it is in: it patrols that room and fights what comes into it, and does not follow you. It leaves the squad, so there is room for someone new. The Ant Nest is one place: anywhere in it, they roam all of it. Its roster card says which room it holds

      
COME Calls the held ones back, newest first, as long as there is room in the squad

      
▣ The enemy card above the orders is the creature the gun is locked on (or the nearest): tap it and the picked followers go for that creature until it is dead or they are given another order. Under its numbers, what its race is WEAK to (green) and RESISTS (grey)

      
INDEX On a follower's card: its attacks, its move (an evolved form can pick another) and its full stat sheet. DISMISS leaves it holding the room it is in

In detail — your ultimate and your followers'
YOUR ULTIMATE — THE ARMY SURGE

      
The bar under your health is your own, and it is siphoned from the followers near you — anything within 6 tiles sends a wisp of static across every couple of seconds, and the charge lands when the wisp arrives. So the rate depends on how many are with you, and none in reach means it does not fill at all — the bar reads NO SQUAD IN RANGE. A follower holding a far-off room is not drawn from.

      
Tap the bar when it is full to spend the whole thing on an ARMY SURGE: every follower and clone healed, WILL refilled, its own ultimate charged, and 1.6× damage for 10 seconds. Nothing is siphoned while a surge is running. The ⌁ switch at the right end of the bar turns the siphon off; wisps already in the air still land.


      
FOLLOWER ULTIMATE CHARGE

      
The thin bar below a follower's health bar shows ultimate charge. Fills from kills (+20) and damage dealt (+3/hit). Max: 100. A ▲ symbol appears when fully charged. Double-tap to fire.

In detail — the wheel on every hit
☯ THE ELEMENT WHEEL

      
Two triangles. Fire > Flux > Toxic > Fire and Electric > Core > Ice > Electric. A hit of the element that beats its target’s does 1.3×; the one it beats does 0.7×; ice does nothing to core. It runs on every hit — your followers’, your clones’ and the creatures’ — against anything that has an element.

Followers are your army: each carries an element, a role and a personality, levels from kills and evolves at level 10. Three walk with you; the rest hold rooms. Clones are creatures you grow from their DNA.
FOLLOWERS
Found as eggs on the pads, or recruited at the Crystal for 30 ◆. Long-press one, or tap its card on the squad bar, for its card: its HP and XP, its two equip card slots, INDEX and DISMISS. Every follower card, on the bar and in the roster, carries its health as a mini bar with the numbers in it.
THE SQUAD OF THREE
Three follow you. HOLD posts one in the room it is in; COME calls it back; a new follower with the squad full holds the room it was found in.
CLONES
4× a creature’s health and power, back 20 s after dying. Summon one in the Clone Bay (CLONES on the dock) for DNA and shards.
BONDS
Two who fight side by side bond: stronger together, raging if one falls.


In detail — recruiting, roles, stats and commands      

        Recruiting: a follower is what it was found as. An egg on a post hatches the type and element its label says, free, once: a claimed post goes dark and stays claimed. + RECRUIT in the roster buys one at the Crystal for 200 ◆: a BRAWLER, a MAGE or a TECHNICIAN, of one of the six elements, drawn evenly, with a personality to match its type and a combat trait, a natural trait and a perk. You do not choose any of it, and cannot change it afterwards: you level it and evolve it. An evolved form also has a subclass (crustacean, mollusk, elemental, drone or jelly), shown in the roster; for now it is only a tag.
      


      
Breeding: two followers that have bonded can breed in the roster (BREED WITH …, 50 ◆, two taps): the offspring comes out at the Crystal with one of its parents' elements (an even chance) and any of the three classes (an even chance, whatever its parents are), raised a little better than a recruit (+5% on its base stats, never worse).

LEVELS

      
Every unit has a level and stats, shown over its health bar as e.g. Lv 12 FIRE BRAWLER, Lv 8 ANT SCOUT, Lv 6 ANT CLONE. A unit's stats are its level-5 base scaled by its level (×2 at 20, ×4 at 50, ×7.3 at 100). A follower's base is the standard stats × its personality; HP, attack, defense, special attack, accuracy and will grow with level (walking speed does not). A creature's base is its species and class, plus armour by class (beetles have extra). A clone is its species with ×4 the health and ×1.5 the power. XP comes from kills: the killer gets 60% and your units within 4 tiles share 40%; your squad shares it wherever they are — a kill by you or a squad member feeds the whole squad of three, so a technician hanging back still levels. Total XP for a level is its cube (Lv 10 = 1,000). A unit that dies keeps its level but loses a quarter of its progress to the next. Cap: level 100. Open a follower's card and press INDEX for its full stat sheet.


      
COMMANDING FOLLOWERS

      
ALL — the orders go to every follower in the squad; tap a card on the squad bar and they go to that one alone

      
RUN (on a creature's card; RETREAT on a phone) — they break off and fall back to you for six seconds

      
HOLD — each holds the room it is in (it patrols it and leaves the squad); COME — the held ones come back while there is room

      
Tap the enemy card → the picked followers attack that creature. The roster's card has the same orders, and FOLLOW ME to call one back.

      
EXILE — on every follower in the ROSTER (the window with all your creatures): it lets that follower go for good, to make room for the next one you find. It asks first: tap again within 4 seconds. Nothing is given back.


In detail — clones
CLONE SUMMONING

      
Clones are the Mutator's. Only a MUTATOR grows them (the class you pick after the tutorial), and one at a time: its clone follows it, takes the squad's orders and fights beside it.

      
A clone is a better version of what you killed. It fights with 4× the health and 1.5× the power of its species and class, as that creature does: its bite in two beats (it rears back, then bites), its special and, for a striker or a tank of the species that have one, its shot. It levels like a follower.

      
It does not come back by itself. When the Mutator's clone falls, its body stays where it fell and a new clone has to be grown. (A clone of the older rule, when every class could clone, respawns at the Crystal after 20 seconds, with a green countdown at the Crystal.)

      
Telling them apart: an allied creature carries a green health bar with a green outline. On the ordinary bar the colour means health; on the ally bar the colour means whose it is and the length still means health.

      
      

      
Summon them from the CLONE BAY, in the Experiment Lab: its glass test chamber (on a phone, the radial menu). A row is ready when you are a Mutator, you have the DNA its species needs and the shards its class costs, and your clone is not already out. Once out, a clone is on the roster like a follower: pick it, give it the squad's orders, read its card.


      
In detail — bonds
♥ BONDS

      
Two followers who fight side by side (within 2.5 tiles) for about 20s bond — a dotted pink line with a heart joins them. One partner each.

      
TogetherWithin 3 tiles of each other: +15% damage, −10% damage taken.

      
RageIf one dies, the other rages for 10s: +50% damage, 1.3× speed, a red ring underfoot. Then the bond is over — the fallen one comes back unbonded.

      
DuoBoth ultimate bars full and the pair close: double-tap either and both ultimates fire at once, and the pair hits 1.3× harder for 5s.

      
In detail — evolution
◆ EVOLUTION — AT LEVEL 10

      
A follower that reaches level 10 and has a form for its element and role evolves on the spot: it molts — the egg shakes and cracks, the glass bursts and its legs drop, and the machine rises out of it. It keeps that form for good, through death and a reload, and its role is fixed from then on. Its health and power are multiplied by 1.25.

      
An evolved form has a signature move it fires by itself when an enemy is near — a ring of fire, five crystal quills at once, a vortex that drags enemies in, a gravity slam — and some forms can choose a second one on the INDEX tab of their card. The technicians' forms (the Goo Proxy, the Thermite Burrower, the Igloo Skater, the Blight Spider, the Hover Shell), the ice mage's (the Rimestar) and the core forms (the Jelly Ram, the Hornshell, the Hammer Slug) have none yet. Two Mages' everyday shots have names: the Ember Hood's FIREBALL, and the Thunderhead's CHAIN LIGHTNING, which conducts on from the enemy it strikes to the next one near enough, weaker each jump. The Thermite Burrower's everyday hit is BURROW STRIKE: it dives, travels under the floor to an enemy it can see within 4 tiles (nothing can touch it there) and erupts beneath it. A technician in your squad has an ability you use from the chip on its card (60 s after it joins the squad, then 60 s to recharge after each use): the electric technician's KINETIC LAUNCHER throws a follower you pick at an enemy it can see (the enemy takes 3× the follower's power, the follower a set 10), and at a launcher site it sets a launcher up for good: the arena's, under the catwalks, throws followers up to the Arena Catwalk; the Sewers', by the collapsed tunnel, carries you too (tap its tag twice), and the floor's broken edge drops you back; the core technician's PHOTONIC SHIELD puts a wall of light 3 tiles wide between it and a creature for 20 s (enemies cannot cross it or hit across it; your side walks and shoots through); the fire technician's THERMITE DRILL drills an enemy three times, and the rubble in the arena's south rock (press and hold it) six times, into a dark hole, sealed for now, with three rocks in front of it (the choke across the Sewers' collapsed tunnel the same way, from the ledge, which opens the way in), and the evolved fire technician, the Thermite Burrower, has a second chip just above the drill's, FAULTLINE: tap it, then press on the floor, draw a path (up to 8 tiles) and let go — it goes under, follows your path, and molten spikes erupt along it, hitting each enemy on the path once (its own 60 s, apart from the drill's); the ice technician's FROST PATCH freezes 3×3 tiles for 10 s (enemies at half speed, your units faster); the toxic technician's BLIGHT SPIDER leaves a poison pool (up to three) that hurts enemies and fades over two in-game days; the flux technician's FLUX MOVE moves one of the arena's loose crates, barrels or barriers up to 2 tiles: tap the object, tap where it goes on its grid, then ✓ PLACE (a barrier turns with ⟳ ROTATE). A Mutator's mutagen sample raises a follower a level toward it.

In detail — perks and traits      

        Each follower has one perk, one combat trait, and one natural trait, drawn when it is found. These are permanent and cannot be changed.
      


      
PERKS (passive stat modifiers)

      

        
Iron Will

        
All incoming damage is reduced by 25% (multiplied by 0.75 before applying). Best on frontline Brawlers and Technicians who take constant hits.

      

      

        
Siphon

        
Heals for 10% of every point of damage dealt. Self-sustaining on high-damage followers. Pairs extremely well with Berserker combat trait — dealing more damage heals more.

      


      
COMBAT TRAITS (active behavior)

      

        
Berserker

        
When the follower's HP drops below 30% of max, all damage output multiplied by 1.5×. Dangerous but volatile — low-HP followers die fast without support. Best with Siphon perk or Aggressive personality.

      

      

        
Opportunistic

        
Automatically re-targets the lowest-health enemy rather than the nearest. Excellent for finishing off wounded enemies and snowballing kills. Pairs well with Mages, who have the range to pick targets.

      


      
NATURAL TRAITS (idle behavior)

      

        
Empathetic

        
Prefers staying near other allied followers when idle. Creates natural clustering — good for coordinated pushes and mutual defense. Empathetic followers are easier to keep together.

      

      

        
Lone Wolf

        
When idle (no combat orders), wanders up to 4 tiles away from the player on its own. Can patrol or scout but may stray into danger. Harder to keep in formation.

      


      
RESONANCE SYSTEM

      
RESONANCE (0–100) builds during combat: +3 per hit, +20 per kill.

      
At 100 it bursts and resets. Wild personality starts with 1.5× resonance multiplier — fills fastest.

      
Electric ultimate grants +30 resonance instantly to all followers around.



    


    
    


The facility's wild things live in the arena: six kinds, each in a class of its own — worker, scout, striker or tank. They potter about from spot to spot and hunt only when provoked or when an alarm is up; then they go for you, your squad and the Crystal. Hit one and it comes for you, and in a fight they pick on the weak: a hurt follower, or you on your last hit. Each bite is telegraphed: step out of reach while it rears back and it misses.
THE SPECIES
ANT
The weakest. Plain attackers: they walk up and bite. The first DNA for the Clone Bay.
BEETLE
Armoured and slow, three times an ant’s health. Fire and flux hit the shell hardest.
SCORPION
Stingers that strike from range from the striker class up.
MANTIS
Long foreleg lunges, and fast to answer a hit.
MOTH
Fliers. A cloud of blinding wing-dust from range; the toughest kind.
SPIDER
Venom shots that poison. The fastest of the big ones.
THE ARENA AND THE ALARM
THE ARENA
Four creatures at the start; another every 20 s, up to 7 alive, always the kind there are fewest of. Scrap metal where one falls.
THE ALARM
The red triangle at the top right. A PROXIMITY ALARM wakes what is near; a FACILITY BREACH wakes everything. It fades 10 s after the last trigger.
A KILL PAYS
Shards on the spot (its worth floats over it), DNA splices for the Clone Bay, scrap for the Armory, and XP for the squad.
In detail — how the creatures behave
CALM, PROVOKED, HUNTING

      
They wander. A calm creature potters about the arena from spot to spot, never out of it. It hunts when it is provoked — shot at, or set on by your squad — or when an alarm is up; under an alarm the creatures it reached hunt whatever is nearest, and the Crystal is what they go for. When the alarm fades a creature nobody is fighting goes back to its round. Between alarms the Crystal heals slowly — 0.6 a second, about eight minutes from empty; it does not heal while an alarm is up.

      
Levels. A creature's stats are its species and class scaled by its level, the way your units' are, with armour by class (beetles have extra). Its level and type show over its health bar; INFO on the dial or on the long-press ring reads its numbers, and the INDEX there describes its kind.

      
The tutorial's assault comes from the east end of the hallway: a countdown, then scouts, then strikers, hunting the Crystal under an alarm until it is over. A Crystal about to fall is saved, mended, and the assault comes again.
