ULTIMATE
Crystal Chamber
▣ GAME INDEX
Conduit in one line: the facility is dark and its power is down. Find supplies, bring the relays back online room by room, gather a squad of followers, and keep the Crystal alive against what comes out of the arena.
HOW A GAME GOES
1
Find supplies in the dark
The facility opens in a blackout; your monitor lamp is your light. Supply cases glow: walk over one to take it. Press I for your pack.
2
Restore a relay
Each relay powers a few rooms. Walk up to a broken one with the relay kit and two power cells (a Technomancer needs one cell and no kit) and it is repaired: its rooms light up.
3
Gather a squad
Three followers walk with you. Hatch the eggs on the pads around the facility, or buy a recruit at the Crystal in the ROSTER for 30 ◆. The rest hold rooms.
4
Hunt the arena
Tap a creature and your gun locks on; click or hold the round dial to fire. A kill pays crystals on the spot and drops DNA for the Clone Bay and scrap for the Armory.
5
Level, evolve, clone
Followers level from kills and evolve at level 10. Spend DNA and crystals in the CLONE BAY on a creature of your own.
6
Defend the Crystal
An alarm sends the creatures hunting, and they go for the Crystal. HOLD followers near it. If the Crystal falls, the game is over.
THE THREE MOVES
TAP THE FLOOR
Walk there, through the doors. Tap a station or a case to use it or take it.
TAP A CREATURE
Lock your gun on it; the round dial fires. Tap the enemy card to send the squad at it.
LONG-PRESS A FOLLOWER
Its card: numbers, INDEX and DISMISS. Long-press a creature for INFO or ATTACK.
In detail — the tutorial and the classes
THE OBJECTIVE PLATE
The plate at the top right (on a phone, a small envelope that wears a ! when there is something new; tap it to unfold it) says what to do next: find supplies, restore the Crystal Chamber's relay, recruit a follower, post two followers near the Crystal, fire your gun, defend the Crystal against a first assault, evolve a follower. The game is saved every few seconds; SETTINGS → NEW GAME throws the save away.
THE CLASSES
When the tutorial is done you choose a class, its supply cases arrive, and a last objective sends you to its station for its perk. Any class may carry another class's gear, but only the right class may use it.
GUNSLINGERWeapons, ammunition and tactical gear. Flares to light the dark; the Armory's machine gun; CCTV sweeps from the Security Room's console that mark every enemy in the facility for a while.
TECHNOMANCERElectrical infrastructure. Repairs a relay with one power cell, by standing at it; a tuned lamp from the console in Teleport Room 1; the spider mech built in the Show Room's mech bay.
MUTATORBiological samples. A mutagen sample raises the nearest follower a level (two once the lab's sequencer has been used); the lab coat, put on by walking through the lab.
The Armory's terminal (once the Armory relay is online) turns scrap metal from the arena into ammunition, equips a weapon you have, and upgrades or assembles one: the service gun everyone starts with, the Gunslinger's machine gun, and a laser rifle that runs on fuel cells.
Everything is a tap: the floor to walk, a creature to aim, a follower's card for its orders. The long-press ring is for a creature (INFO, ATTACK) and for your followers (their card).

In detail — your ultimate and your followers'
YOUR ULTIMATE — THE ARMY SURGE
The bar under your health is your own, and it is siphoned from the followers near you — anything within 6 tiles sends a wisp of static across every couple of seconds, and the charge lands when the wisp arrives. So the rate depends on how many are with you, and none in reach means it does not fill at all — the bar reads NO SQUAD IN RANGE. A follower holding a far-off room is not drawn from.
Tap the bar when it is full to spend the whole thing on an ARMY SURGE: every follower and clone healed, WILL refilled, its own ultimate charged, and 1.6× damage for 10 seconds. Nothing is siphoned while a surge is running. The ⌁ switch at the right end of the bar turns the siphon off; wisps already in the air still land.
FOLLOWER ULTIMATE CHARGE
The thin bar below a follower's health bar shows ultimate charge. Fills from kills (+20) and damage dealt (+3/hit). Max: 100. A ▲ symbol appears when fully charged. Double-tap to fire.
Six elements, all of them yours from the start. Each one colours a follower and decides what its attacks do. A follower's element is the one it was found with: an egg's pad says it, and a recruit at the Crystal draws one of the six.

In detail — the wheel on every hit
☯ THE ELEMENT WHEEL
Two triangles. Fire > Flux > Toxic > Fire and Electric > Core > Ice > Electric. A hit of the element that beats its target’s does 1.3×; the one it beats does 0.7×; ice does nothing to core. It runs on every hit — your followers’, your clones’ and the creatures’ — against anything that has an element.
In detail — follower attacks by element
FIRE Proc: 30%
Physical — Ring of Fire: 0.4× ATK dmg to all enemies within 1.5 tiles.
Special — Fire Bombs: two bouncing bombs lobbed at the target, 0.7× SP.ATK splash each where they land, and the ground burns for 10–15 s.
Ultimate — Volcanic Eruption: the ground cracks under the biggest enemy within 12 tiles and a column of fire erupts. On impact: 3.5× SP.ATK dmg in a 5.5-tile radius (falls off with distance). Leaves a flame crater that burns for 5 s.
Burn proc (30%): DoT for ~2s on physical hits, ~3s on special hits. Extended burn applied by ultimates.
ELECTRIC Proc: 40%
Physical — Chain Lightning: 0.35× ATK primary hit, chains to up to 3 targets within 3 tiles. Each jump loses 20% power.
Special — Static Field: 0.7× SP.ATK to all enemies in 3.0-tile radius. High stun proc rate.
Ultimate — EMP: Instantly destroys all enemy shields around. Grants the followers around +30 resonance and 1.5× speed for 5 seconds.
Arc Flash proc (40%): Stuns target briefly. Highest proc rate of all elements.
ICE Proc: 8%
Physical — Frost Lance: 0.55× ATK single-target. Hit chance = 60% + ACC/50 (max 98%). Proc blocked by active shields.
Special — Ice Burst: 0.6× SP.ATK in 2.5-tile radius. Applies slow (40% speed) for 3s. Freeze/slow blocked by shields.
Ultimate — Blizzard: 8s freeze field around it. Every 0.5s: 60% freeze chance on each enemy in it. Frozen enemies can escape each frame (very slowly).
Hard Freeze proc (8%): Rare but powerful — target fully stops. Cannot freeze shielded enemies.
FLUX Proc: 15%
Physical — Volatility Aura: 0.6× ATK + disorient proc. Pierces shields — disorient always applies regardless of shield status.
Special — Singularity: Creates a gravity well at target. Pulls enemies within 3 tiles inward (0.8-tile yank). Deals 0.5× SP.ATK and applies disorient. Pierces shields.
Ultimate — Disorient: All creatures around enter friendly-fire mode for 4s — they attack each other at 0.5× power.
Disorient proc (15%): Target attacks randomly (including allies) for 2s. Bypasses shields.
CORE Proc: 30%
Physical — Shield Bash: 0.45× ATK + grants caster a defense boost for 1.5s. Proc knocks target 1.5 tiles back.
Special — Radiant Pulse: Caster becomes invulnerable for 1s. Shields all allies within 3.0 tiles (shield HP = 1.5× SP.ATK). Also deals 0.4× SP.ATK to enemies in range.
Ultimate — Shield Grant: Every follower on the entire map instantly receives a 10 HP shield.
Knockback proc (30%): Physically launches target 1.5 tiles away from attacker.
TOXIC Proc: 50%
Physical — Smoke Form: Caster enters 75% evasion for ~0.75s while striking for 0.4× ATK. High proc rate shreds target defense.
Special — Toxic Cloud: Drops a 1.8-tile lingering cloud at target. Deals tick damage every 0.33s for ~5s. Persists on the ground.
Ultimate — Smoke Screen: 8s fog around it. All enemies in it suffer −50% accuracy for the duration.
Defense Shred proc (50%): Cuts target's defense by 50% for 2s. Highest proc rate of all elements.

In detail — recruiting, roles, stats and commands
Recruiting: a follower is what it was found as. An egg on a pad hatches the type and element its label says, and the pad fills again a while after you have walked away. + RECRUIT in the roster buys one at the Crystal for 30 ◆: a BRAWLER, a MAGE or a TECHNICIAN, of one of the six elements, drawn evenly, with a personality to match its type and a combat trait, a natural trait and a perk. You do not choose any of it, and cannot change it afterwards: you level it and evolve it.
ROLES
A follower's role is its type, fixed when it is found. An evolved form keeps it.

Brawler — Aggressive melee fighter. Chases enemies directly, closes distance fast.
Personality: Aggressive or Wild · Best with the Berserker combat trait
M
Mage — Ranged caster. Keeps its distance from targets, casts special attacks.
Personality: Cautious or Cunning · Best with the Opportunistic combat trait
T
Technician — Holds its ground and defends it at short range. Doesn't advance far; the one to leave holding a room.

LEVELS
Every unit has a level and stats, shown over its health bar as e.g. Lv 12 FIRE BRAWLER, Lv 8 ANT SCOUT, Lv 6 ANT CLONE. A unit's stats are its level-5 base scaled by its level (×2 at 20, ×4 at 50, ×7.3 at 100). A follower's base is the standard stats × its personality; HP, attack, defense, special attack, accuracy and will grow with level (walking speed does not). A creature's base is its species and class, plus armour by class (beetles have extra). A clone is its species with ×4 the health and ×1.5 the power. XP comes from kills: the killer gets 60% and your units within 4 tiles share 40%. Total XP for a level is its cube (Lv 10 = 1,000). A unit that dies keeps its level but loses a quarter of its progress to the next. Cap: level 100. Open a follower's card and press INDEX for its full stat sheet.
COMMANDING FOLLOWERS
ALL — the orders go to every follower in the squad; tap a card on the squad bar and they go to that one alone
RETREAT — they break off and fall back to you for six seconds
HOLD — each holds the room it is in (it patrols it and leaves the squad); COME — the held ones come back while there is room
Tap the enemy card → the picked followers attack that creature. The roster's card has the same orders, and FOLLOW ME to call one back.
In detail — clones
CLONE SUMMONING
Clones are the Mutator's. Only a MUTATOR grows them (the class you pick after the tutorial), and one at a time: its clone follows it, takes the squad's orders and fights beside it.
A clone is a better version of what you killed. It fights with 4× the health and 1.5× the power of its species and class, as that creature does: its bite in two beats (it rears back, then bites), its special and, for a striker or a tank of the species that have one, its shot. It levels like a follower.
It does not come back by itself. When the Mutator's clone falls, its body stays where it fell and a new clone has to be grown. (A clone of the older rule, when every class could clone, respawns at the Crystal after 20 seconds, with a green countdown at the Crystal.)
Telling them apart: an allied creature carries a green health bar with a green outline. On the ordinary bar the colour means health; on the ally bar the colour means whose it is and the length still means health.
Summon them from the CLONE BAY, opened by CLONES on the dock (on a phone, the radial menu). A row is ready when you are a Mutator, you have the DNA its species needs and the crystals its class costs, and your clone is not already out. Once out, a clone is on the roster like a follower: pick it, give it the squad's orders, read its card.
In detail — bonds
♥ BONDS
Two followers who fight side by side (within 2.5 tiles) for about 20s bond — a dotted pink line with a heart joins them. One partner each.
TogetherWithin 3 tiles of each other: +15% damage, −10% damage taken.
RageIf one dies, the other rages for 10s: +50% damage, 1.3× speed, a red ring underfoot. Then the bond is over — the fallen one comes back unbonded.
DuoBoth ultimate bars full and the pair close: double-tap either and both ultimates fire at once, and the pair hits 1.3× harder for 5s.
In detail — evolution
◆ EVOLUTION — AT LEVEL 10
A follower that reaches level 10 and has a form for its element and role evolves on the spot: it molts — the egg shakes and cracks, the glass bursts and its legs drop, and the machine rises out of it. It keeps that form for good, through death and a reload, and its role is fixed from then on. Its health and power are multiplied by 1.25.
An evolved form has a signature move it fires by itself when an enemy is near — a ring of fire, a charged arc that jumps between enemies, a gravity slam, a shield burst — and some forms can choose a second one on the INDEX tab of their card. A Mutator's mutagen sample raises a follower a level toward it.

In detail — perks and traits
Each follower has one perk, one combat trait, and one natural trait, drawn when it is found. These are permanent and cannot be changed.
PERKS (passive stat modifiers)
Iron Will
All incoming damage is reduced by 25% (multiplied by 0.75 before applying). Best on frontline Brawlers and Technicians who take constant hits.
Siphon
Heals for 10% of every point of damage dealt. Self-sustaining on high-damage followers. Pairs extremely well with Berserker combat trait — dealing more damage heals more.
COMBAT TRAITS (active behavior)
Berserker
When the follower's HP drops below 30% of max, all damage output multiplied by 1.5×. Dangerous but volatile — low-HP followers die fast without support. Best with Siphon perk or Aggressive personality.
Opportunistic
Automatically re-targets the lowest-health enemy rather than the nearest. Excellent for finishing off wounded enemies and snowballing kills. Pairs well with Mages, who have the range to pick targets.
NATURAL TRAITS (idle behavior)
Empathetic
Prefers staying near other allied followers when idle. Creates natural clustering — good for coordinated pushes and mutual defense. Empathetic followers are easier to keep together.
Lone Wolf
When idle (no combat orders), wanders up to 4 tiles away from the player on its own. Can patrol or scout but may stray into danger. Harder to keep in formation.
RESONANCE SYSTEM
RESONANCE (0–100) builds during combat: +3 per hit, +20 per kill.
At 100 it bursts and resets. Wild personality starts with 1.5× resonance multiplier — fills fastest.
Electric ultimate grants +30 resonance instantly to all followers around.

THE ARENA AND THE ALARM
THE ARENA
Four creatures at the start; another every 20 s, up to 7 alive, always the kind there are fewest of. Scrap metal where one falls.
THE ALARM
The red triangle at the top right. A PROXIMITY ALARM wakes what is near; a FACILITY BREACH wakes everything. It fades 10 s after the last trigger.

Crystals on the spot (its worth floats over it), DNA splices for the Clone Bay, scrap for the Armory, and XP for the squad.
In detail — how the creatures behave
CALM, PROVOKED, HUNTING
They wander. A calm creature potters about the arena from spot to spot, never out of it. It hunts when it is provoked — shot at, or set on by your squad — or when an alarm is up; under an alarm the creatures it reached hunt whatever is nearest, and the Crystal is what they go for. When the alarm fades a creature nobody is fighting goes back to its round. Between alarms the Crystal heals slowly — 0.6 a second, about eight minutes from empty; it does not heal while an alarm is up.
Levels. A creature's stats are its species and class scaled by its level, the way your units' are, with armour by class (beetles have extra). Its level and type show over its health bar; INFO on the dial or on the long-press ring reads its numbers, and the INDEX there describes its kind.
The tutorial's assault comes from the east end of the hallway: a countdown, then scouts, then strikers, hunting the Crystal under an alarm until it is over. A Crystal about to fall is saved, mended, and the assault comes again.
