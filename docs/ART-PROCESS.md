# Art process: no blind mock-up sheets

Learned the hard way (the equipment redesign went through nine sheets and none of them landed): **do not draw art guesses to find the style.** Each sheet costs the user credits and the style words alone ("ancient angel", "Star Fox angular", "N64") were not enough to hit what they see in their head.

Before drawing anything for the game's look:

1. **Ask for a reference** (a screenshot, a game and which part of it, a picture from anywhere) or for a short written spec of what the piece is and how it should read. Do not infer a style from one or two words.
2. **One piece first** (one prop or one form, large, one version), never a sheet of six directions. Get a yes or a specific correction before the rest of its family is drawn.
3. **Only then** build it in the game, and keep the old art until the new one is approved.

What is already decided and shipped: the Crystal Laboratory style of the facility's walls, doors, glass and floors (docs/STYLE-CRYSTAL-LAB.md), the props as 3D models baked to sprites in the game's own projection (design/props/BRIEF.md is the modelling brief; docs/TUTORIAL.md, "3D props"), and the followers' evolved forms baked the same way (design/evolutions). The nine mock sheets were for equipment that is no longer in the game: nothing from them was chosen, and nothing from them is needed.
