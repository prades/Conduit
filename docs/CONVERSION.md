## 2. Approved design (2026-11-02)

### BLOOM
- **A seventh element**, beside fire, toxic, electric, ice, flux and core: life-giving, a medical substance made from toxin with the poison filtered out.
- **Where it comes from:** only the conversion apparatus, by breeding and by converting a toxic follower. Never from recruits, the posts' eggs or enemies.
- **Its match-ups:** outside the two triangles. No element beats it and it beats none: ×1 both ways (`COMBAT.md` §7).
- **Its status, MEND:** every BLOOM hit heals the most hurt ally within 4 tiles by 2% of that ally's max health. BLOOM creatures have no other trait: the owner passed over Regrowth, Seed on fall, Purify and Thorns.
- **Its colour:** blossom pink, `#ff7ab8`.
- **Its forms:** a BLOOM Brawler, Mage and Technician, designed one at a time for the owner's approval. Until a class has its BLOOM form, the class draw skips that class (below).

### The conversion apparatus
- **It is the purification module itself** (the owner, 2026-11-08: "the purification module within the sewers, after it's been installed by the player, is the place where if the player brings a toxic creature on their squad, or a toxic creature is holding the area, then the player can interact with the machine"). *(Superseded: a separate apparatus beside the module, handed over free when the quest is finished, 2026-11-02.)*
- **When:** once the player has installed the module at the console and its cycle has run (the water clean: the quest's `installedAt` and `purifiedAt`). While the cycle runs, during the queen's fight, the mount still says THE MODULE IS RUNNING. *(The agent's reading of "after it's been installed", build `2026-11-08i`; confirmed by the owner, 2026-11-08: "Your calls were correct".)*
- **Who can use it:** the player, when a toxic follower is in the squad or holding the Sewers (posted there).
- **Using it** (the owner's flow, 2026-11-08):
  1. The player interacts with the machine: a pop-up opens with a roster view of the toxic followers that are there (in the squad or holding the Sewers; the owner chose "Only toxic ones there"). The player scrolls through them and picks one.
  2. A second pop-up shows that follower's stats, the stat conversion, and the roster card of the creature it would become. The stats do not change (the owner chose "Same stats, show changes"): they are shown side by side, before and after, with what does change marked: its element (toxic to BLOOM), its trait (its toxic status to MEND), its form, its moves and its ability.
- **Built in stages** (the owner chose "Stages, menus first"): first the machine, its rule of use, both pop-ups and the real conversion of an unevolved toxic follower (it becomes a BLOOM follower with MEND); an evolved one shows that its BLOOM form is not ready. The Mage, the Little Shrub, came in build `2026-11-08j` (an evolved toxic Mage converts into it), and SUNDEW in build `2026-11-08k` (an evolved toxic Brawler converts into it); the coral crab comes next.
- **It filters the toxin out** ("detox can be filtered out and create new life creatures"). With it installed:
  - **Breeding:** a bonded toxic + fire pair's offspring is BLOOM **half the time** (50%).
  - **Conversion:** a **toxic** follower can be converted into a **BLOOM** follower at the apparatus.
- **A conversion is free and keeps everything:** the follower's level, XP, bond and stats.
  - An evolved follower takes its class's BLOOM form.
  - Its toxic cards come off.
  - It cannot be undone.

## 7. Edge cases and failure behavior

- **A parent already BLOOM:** the draw is between BLOOM and the other parent's element.
- **Toxic + fire before the apparatus:** the plain draw, toxic or fire.
- **Converting an evolved toxic follower:** it takes its class's BLOOM form. Until that form exists, the apparatus refuses, saying the form is not ready. A toxic egg, or a follower whose class has its BLOOM form, can always convert (the owner, 2026-11-02).
- **Converting a follower that wears toxic element cards:** they come off. They go back as when any card stops fitting.
- **A BLOOM offspring's class draw** skips any class without a BLOOM form. With no BLOOM form yet, BLOOM breeding cannot go live, so at least one form comes first.

## 9. Implementation notes

- **The breeding draws** (build `2026-11-02f`): js/breeding.js `breedElement(a, b, draw)` (under 0.5 the first parent's element, else the second's) and `breedClass(draw)` (an even draw over `FOLLOWER_TYPE_ORDER`). `breedMake(f, rnd)` takes every draw from one source, in the order element, class, then the stats' variations, so a test's seeded `rnd` gives the same offspring's element and class every time. The lead-parent rule (`breedLead`, `breedType`) is gone.
- **BLOOM and the conversion's first stage (build `2026-11-08i`):**
  - **The element:** js/config.js `ELEMENTS` has BLOOM (id `blossom`, label BLOOM, `#ff7ab8`, `converted: true`); `elementsFound()` is every element but BLOOM and fills `unlockedElements`, the pool of every random pick (recruits, eggs, creatures), so BLOOM is never found. Outside the triangles it is ×1 both ways (js/elements.js, no table entry). An unevolved follower is named by its element's label (js/levels.js `unitTypeLabel`: BLOOM BRAWLER).
  - **MEND** (js/elements.js `elementMend`, `ELEMENT_MEND` radius 4, share 0.02; called from js/helpers.js `applyDamage` on any damaging hit by a BLOOM follower): the most hurt of your followers within 4 tiles, by the lowest share of health left, the BLOOM follower itself included (*the agent's reading of "the most hurt ally"; confirmed by the owner, 2026-11-08: "Your calls were correct"*); a follower at full health is passed over.
  - **What an unevolved BLOOM follower has:** a plain hit (no element move: js/combat.js skips an element with none), no Ultimate, and it does not evolve while its class's BLOOM form is not in the game (js/evolution.js `evolutionFor` finds none: it waits at its level).
