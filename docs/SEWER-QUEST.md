# The Sewer purification quest line: the sample puzzle, the quest line, the Toxic Queen, Side Quest and the Sleeping Potion

Built 2026-10-31 (builds `2026-10-31c` to `2026-10-31f`) from the "Series 2 cards, sewer exploration, puzzle systems, non-lethal gameplay, boss domains and quest integration" brief, then from the Game Design Archive's "Next Major Update Framework" page as it stood at the end of that day (its patch frame, its clarifications on Side Quest, the Toxic Queen's targeting and the sleeping potion, its quest-line terminology). Code: `js/puzzles.js` (the puzzle's definition and its interaction contracts), `js/quests.js` (the quest line), `js/bosses.js` (the boss encounters and the Toxic Queen's rules), `js/potion.js` (the Sleeping Potion), `js/sidequest.js` (Side Quest), the Sewers' map data in `js/facilitymap.js`, the brood in `js/facilityspawn.js` (`FP_SPAWN_SEWERS`), the stations in `js/stations.js`, the items in `js/inventory.js`, the statuses in `js/combat.js`, the water's colour and the open gates in `js/facilitysewers.js`. Tests: `tests/sewerquest.js`, `tests/potion.js`, `tests/sidequest.js` (and the counts in `tests/sewers.js`, `tests/techabilities.js`, `tests/techlevel1.js`, `tests/market.js`, `tests/facilityplay.js`).

**The term is quest line** for the whole sequence (the archive, 2026-10-31); a step of it is a step or an objective. The user's standing instruction: Side Quest's line after the Toxic Queen's fate is decided is the placeholder "…", whether she is spared or killed.

Decided with the user before building (2026-10-31): the Sewers' boss is **a toxic ant boss** named **THE TOXIC ANT QUEEN** (after the archive's proposed faction progression; the archive's newest notes call her "the Toxic Queen"; the name is provisional); the puzzle's second solution was **a fire technician launched into the tunnel, which drills the choke open**; the non-lethal end is **the boss calmed, alive, keeping her post**; Series 2 is **Booster Pack 2** with the archive's approved rules, and a spared boss counts for its unlock. The archive has **no Series 2 card list** and no boss numbers: both remain open.

**The puzzle was redone later the same day (build `2026-10-31j`)** at the owner's word: "the player can use one of three options and doesn't have to rely on two of the techniques used together, rather just one. So you can either telegrab the item, or you can use a launch pad, and the launch pad is supposed to launch the player as well, and you could pick it up that way and then jump down or to the main platform from there [...] it should be basically be a broken off part of the sewer system. And then the final way to get it is to break apart a piece of rubble or whatever, and you can walk there yourself. Redesign the tunnel a little bit so it's wider and can accommodate a puzzle platform like this." So the launcher now carries the player (the archive's jump pad after all), the fire technician drills from the ledge, Telegrab stands, and the tunnel is a wide broken-off floor.

## The Sewers now

What was a `collapse` wall decoration on the north ledge is a **collapsed tunnel**: a **broken-off stretch of an old side tunnel's floor**, six tiles across and four deep (x 820 to 940, y 780 to 860 in the sketch), open onto the ledge along its whole width (so all of it is in plain sight; a wall would hide it) and shut off by a **choke** of rubble along that whole mouth (`sewer_choke`, a solid of kind `rubble` lying a hair inside the mouth's line, with `rubble_sewer_choke` in the map's `rubble` list: `solid: "sewer_choke"`, its `a`–`b` the six tiles of the mouth, its **work spot on the ledge** in front of the pile's middle; no `inside`). At the floor's far (east) end lies the **WATER SAMPLE** vial (`pk_sewer_sample`), at its west end the **Vital Fleck** (`pk_vital_tunnel`). On the ledge a tile west of the mouth is a **pocket launcher site that carries the player** (`site_sewer_tunnel`: `pocket: true`, `rider: true`, no `only`), with a **broken edge** at the floor's west end (`drop`, with `dropTo` on the ledge by the pad). The rubble is drawn as the pile over and over along the six tiles (`techDrawRubbleOn`, two tiles to a pile). At the head of the cistern's sump stands the **purifier mount** (`purifier_mount`, a console); **the Toxic Queen's post** is beside it, east of it. The boss gate across the south ledge's way into the cistern has a station on it. The **side passage off the south ledge** (x 600 to 640 in the sketch, behind `sewer_gate_s1`, "an area for later") is **Side Quest's**: its gate opens when he appears and he hovers at its end. Nothing of the brood comes up on the tunnel's floor while it is cut off (`facilitySpawnSpot` keeps off a launcher's landing until the floors are one).

## The puzzle (`js/puzzles.js`)

`PUZZLES.sewer_sample` is data: the objects it is made of (by map id), its three solutions, and an **interaction contract** for each step a solution uses: who does it, through which function of the game (`fns`: the names, which the tests check exist), what it needs, the refusals it gives word for word, what it does, and what a retry means. The puzzle has no logic of its own: the technicians' abilities, the pickups and the floor are the game's. `puzzleState(id)` reads the world: `{ solved, how, solvedAt, sampleTaken, chokeOpen, launcherUp, inside, up }` (`inside`: a follower waiting on the floor beyond the choke; `up`: the player is on it while the choke is shut); the solve and how it was done are a record (`_fp.puzzles`, saved as `puzzles`) and are copied into the quest line (`puzzleHow`: `telegrab`, `ride` or `drill`).

**Three ways, each on its own** (one technician, one ability; no two needed together):

- **A, Telegrab** (the flux technician). From the ledge the vial is on the screen, so the chip takes it (`techItemAtScreen`, `techAbilityUse(tech, { item })`): it flies over the rubble to the player's feet and the pickup takes it, once.
- **B, the launcher** (the electric technician). It sets a Kinetic Launcher up on the ledge by the mouth (`techSiteSetup`). **The launcher carries you**: tap its tag once for its aim and again to ride it (`techSiteRide`): you walk to the pad and are thrown the same arc a follower flies (in the air nothing else moves you and no wall or solid stops you: `facilityPlayerStep` steps `techFlightStep` and the draw lifts you), and land on the floor beyond the choke. Walk to the vial and take it. The way back is the floor's **broken edge** (▼ JUMP DOWN on its tag, `techSiteDrop`): you walk to it and drop to the ledge by the launcher, one way; the launcher is the way back up. Followers may be sent over too (`techSiteLaunch`, anyone): one lands and **waits there for you** (`_pocketHold`, still a squad member) until you are up there too, the way is open, or it is called (COME) or ordered; up there without you, it drops from the broken edge after you (`_techDropScan`). The ride is refused, and says why, when there is no launcher yet, the launcher is in use, you are up there already, you are in the air or in a doorway, or there is no way to the pad; a walk elsewhere calls it off, and so does not reaching the pad in 8 s. The arena's site throws followers only ("IT THROWS FOLLOWERS ONLY").
- **C, the drill** (the fire technician). Press and hold the choke and pick THERMITE DRILL (`techRingAct`): the fire technician walks to the work spot **on the ledge** and drills six hits a second apart; the sixth opens the choke's solid for good (`facilitySolidOpen`): the floor joins the ledge, three rocks lie in front of the pile, and everyone walks in. Nothing else is needed. (The old refusal from outside, "IT IS DRILLED FROM INSIDE …", belongs to a choke with `inside: true`; none is so now.) With the way open the broken edge is just floor: the jump is refused ("THE WAY DOWN IS OPEN · WALK").

## The quest line (`js/quests.js`)

The game's quest system is the tutorial director (`js/tutorial.js`): steps with a `done()`, one at a time; each is noted in the dialogue box as it comes up (the player's own note, `js/dialogue.js`) and listed in the QUESTS box (`js/questbox.js`; the objective plate they were on is gone since 2026-11-01). The quest line is the same shape (`QUEST_STEPS.purify`), with a record of **what happened** (`_fp.quests.purify`, saved as `quests`), each state marked once (`questMark`) at the play clock's time and never unmarked; a later step never skips an earlier one.

| State | Marked when | Where |
|---|---|---|
| `discoveredAt` | the player is within `discoverNear` (6 tiles) of the outfall in the Sewers ("◈ WATER-QUALITY READING: TOXIC …") | `questTick` |
| `puzzleAt`, `puzzleHow` | the puzzle's record says solved (`telegrab` / `ride` / `drill`) | `questTick` ← `puzzleState` |
| `samplesAt` | three TOXIC ANT SAMPLES and the WATER SAMPLE are in the pack | `questTick` |
| `turnedInAt` | the ANALYSIS BENCH (the cabinet at the Experiment Lab's west end, `st_analysis_bench`) took all four (ANALYSE) | `questBenchUse` |
| `moduleAt` | the bench sold the PURIFICATION MODULE for **◆300** (refused with fewer; once; `QUEST_PURIFY.modulePrice`: the brief's 800 until build `2026-11-01a`, 300 since, at the owner's word) | `questBenchUse` |
| `gateAt` | the boss gate's seal broke for the module; the gate solid opens, the queen is **armed** asleep at her post | `questGateUse` |
| `installedAt`, `cycleLeft` | the module was mounted on the console (`st_purifier`: a build of `install` = **600 frames, about 10 s**, the archive's number, that the player stays at; starting it **wakes the queen**; walking away stops it, the module is kept); the cycle of `cycle` = 1800 frames begins | `questMountStart`, `questMountUse` |
| `purifiedAt`, `closeLeft` | the cycle ran out: the water is clean; a living queen is **calmed**; the closing line stays `closing` frames | `questTick` |
| `bossStartedAt` | the encounter began (the install, or a hit on the sleeper) | `questBossStarted` ← `bossWake` |
| `bossResolvedAt`, `bossOutcome` | the encounter ended: `calm` or `defeated`, once | `questBossResolved` |
| `bossDefeated` | the queen died, whenever (after a calm too: the outcome stays `calm`) | `questBossResolved` |
| `antKills` | a toxic ant of the Sewers' brood died (`questAntKilled` from `onPredatorDeath`; never the queen, never a straggler thinned quietly): Side Quest's gate with `antTarget` = 10 | `questAntKilled` |

**The samples.** The Sewers' **brood** (`FP_SPAWN_SEWERS`): toxic ants, three the first time the player is in the Sewers and one every 1500 frames while there, up to four, on floor the player can reach, far from the player and never within 3 tiles of Side Quest. A brood ant killed drops a TOXIC ANT SAMPLE while the bench still wants them (`questDropFor`). The water sample is the puzzle's vial.

**The bench**, in the quest line's order: ANALYSE, then BUY THE PURIFICATION MODULE ◆300; after that, with Side Quest's potion in the pack, COPY THE SLEEPING POTION ◆100 (below).

**Non-lethal.** The cycle completes whether the queen lives or not; the water itself clears as the cycle runs (`questWaterClean`, 0 to 1, in `js/facilitysewers.js`). The plate's install hint says what she does and never that she must die; it names Side Quest once he is there.

## The Toxic Queen (`js/bosses.js`)

`BOSS_ENCOUNTERS` is a table: domain (a room), the creature (a species' boss class at a level), its POST (`lair`: where it sleeps, sits and returns to), its leash, the console it keeps (`mount`, `guard`), its ATTACKS, its WAVES, its FRANTIC rule, what wakes it and what calms it. One state machine for every row: **idle** → **armed** (`bossArm`: spawned asleep at its post) → **active** (`bossWake`: the install begins, or it is hit) → **resolved**, `calm` (`bossCalm`) or `defeated`. The creature is the game's own (`spawnCreature`); the framework adds `bossId`, `bossName`, `bossAsleep`, `bossCalm`, `bossRooted`, `bossFrantic`. Asleep or calm it hears no alarm and picks no prey; the auto-lock, a follower's guard and the squad's own targeting pass it by (`bossPassive`).

**The Sewers' row, THE TOXIC ANT QUEEN** (species ant, class boss, element toxic, level 6, **provisional**), her post beside the console, east of it; the rules the archive set on 2026-10-31 (its numbers that are open are **provisional**, marked in the table):

- **Rooted.** Awake and above half her health she sits at her post and **does not chase**: she turns on whoever hits her or comes close (`js/predator.js`: a rooted boss faces its prey and takes no step; neither a rooted nor a frantic boss ever goes for the Crystal). The install **holds** while she is within `guard` = 2.5 tiles of the console and not controlled (`bossInstallBlocked` → the plate: "THE TOXIC ANT QUEEN SITS AT THE CONSOLE · SLEEP, STUN, FREEZE OR GLITCH HER, OR DRIVE HER TO HALF HER HEALTH"); `bossControlled` is a stun, a freeze, a glitch or a sleep. Progress made in one window keeps through the next block.
- **Three attacks, independent timers** (`attacks`). The **BOMB**: every `bomb.every` = 2700 frames (the archive's 45 s) at whoever she is fighting within 12 tiles of her in her room, a wind-up of 60 frames with a ring on the floor where it will land, 60 frames in the air, landing where its target stood when it left her: a player who stands still is caught (radius 1.6), one who moves is not; a follower in it takes 1.5 × her power as toxic damage, you one of your hits. The **SPIT**: only while you are within 3 tiles (the archive's "proximity projectile"), every `spit.cooldown` = 600 frames (its 10 s), 30 frames of wind-up and 30 in the air, a tile's burst. The **BITE**: the creature's own two beats in reach. A **sleep** on her cancels her wind-up, drops what she has in the air and puts **both** timers back to the full (`bossOnSleep`: the archive's "sleep resets both projectile timers"); a stun, freeze or glitch cancels the wind-up and leaves the timers. Her species special (MANDIBLE FRENZY) stays hers.
- **Waves.** While rooted and in combat she calls `waves.n` = 4 weak toxic **nymphs** every `waves.every` = 1800 frames (the archive's 30 s; "start at 4, tune up to 6"), the first 1800 frames after she wakes (provisional), level 2 to 3, up beside her, hunting you; they are brood (the purification thins them with the rest). A nymph's bite **poisons** you (`js/combat.js playerPoison`): for `PLAYER_POISON.frames` = 120 your own hits land at 60% (the game's poison is a weakness, as the toxic cards'), then it is gone; a POISONED pill counts it down beside the ammunition. Back off a moment, come back.
- **Frantic**, once, at `frantic.at` = 50% of her health: the wave timer stops and a wave that was due never comes; she leaves her post and **roams** the cistern (`bossWanderTarget`: within 15 tiles of her post) at 3 times her pace (provisional), chasing what provokes her like any creature; let go beyond her leash (15 tiles from her post, provisional: the whole cistern) she only roams on, and she **never sleeps again**. The plate: "… IS AT THE CONSOLE · LURE HER OFF, OR SLEEP, STUN, FREEZE OR GLITCH HER".
- **The way in** (the archive's beat sheet): lure her off the console, **sleep** her with Side Quest's potion (about 5 s), rush to the console and install (about 10 s) while your followers hold her when she wakes. **A hit on you cancels nothing**: while a build or repair runs a bite's step back is not taken (`js/helpers.js hurtPlayer`), so only walking away ends the install (`js/stations.js facilityRelayTick`).
- **Resolved.** The water clean calms her, alive, at her post (she defends herself if hit and calms again); killed, defeated. A boss killed after it was calmed is recorded without changing the outcome. Either way Booster Pack 2 unlocks (`bossAnyResolved`).

A chaser now faces the way it walks (the route's next waypoint), not its prey: facing the prey behind the sump turned its next step into the sump, and it stood pressed against it for good (`js/predator.js`, found by the frantic queen's chase; it was true of any creature chasing round an obstacle).

**The Nest row** names THE ANT QUEEN (the archive's proposed first nest boss, provisional) with no post, level or numbers; nothing arms it.

**Saved** as `bosses` (state, outcome, started, defeated). A load: armed or active → armed again, asleep at its post, whole (an interrupted encounter begins again); resolved calm and not defeated → a calm queen at her post; defeated → nothing.

## Side Quest and the Sleeping Potion (`js/sidequest.js`, `js/potion.js`)

**Side Quest** is the archive's small flying drone, an outlander who left the facility: an optional side-quest giver, merchant and source of hints (his "gadgets and gizmos" and his later role are open). He appears in the south ledge's side passage **only when the samples have been turned in at the lab AND `antTarget` = 10 toxic ants of the Sewers have been killed** (`sqUnlocked`; not location, not either alone; the archive's "around 10" is tunable). When he appears the passage's gate opens (`facilitySolidOpen`: he is a technician) and he hovers at its end with a STATION on him (`STATION_KINDS.sidequest`, made at runtime by `sqPlace`): the plate, E, a tap, as any station. The brood keeps 3 tiles off him; the queen's roam is 15 tiles of the console, his passage 29 away. The first time you come within 8 tiles he calls you over, once.

**The talk**: four lines, one a press: the archive's suggested opening word for word ("OH NO, IT'S CLOGGING EVERYTHING. LOOK, PLEASE, THERE HAS TO BE A WAY TO FIX THIS WITHOUT KILLING IT."), then who he is and that the Queen is blocking the system, his idea that she only needs to be moved, and the **promise** he asks for; then a choice of two buttons, **I PROMISE** or **NO PROMISES**, in the dialogue box, where he talks with his picture since 2026-11-01 (a tap on his line is NEXT; js/dialogue.js) (`sqChoose`: recorded in `promised`, 1 or −1). The promise changes nothing of the quest line and forbids nothing: killing her later invalidates nothing (the archive). Either way he answers and sells the potion after; come back and he gives the hint. **Once the Queen's fate is decided** (the quest line's `bossResolvedAt`, calm or defeated) everything he says is **"…"** (the user's placeholder) and he still sells. He never suggests copying his potion.

**The Sleeping Potion** (`ITEMS.sleeping_potion`, the original; `ITEMS.sleeping_potion_copy`, the lab's copy): tools of the pack whose button **EQUIPS** them (one kind in hand at a time). Equipped, the **POTION pill** stands beside the ammunition for every class and the key **T** is the same: either **throws** it (the serum's own model, `js/serum.js`: the pack's button, a pill, a key; a throw instead of a splash at the feet). It flies 20 frames and shatters at your feet, or on the creature your dial has locked within 4 tiles in the same room (a lob over the console). Where it shatters, everyone eligible within **3 tiles** sleeps (`applySleep`, `js/combat.js`) for **300 frames** (the archive's "about 5 seconds" on the Queen; provisional for every other creature): the **original** takes enemies and your followers and clones, **never you**; the **copy** enemies only, for a quarter of the sleep (75 frames: the archive's "one-fourth effectiveness", read as the sleep's length). A sleeper takes no step and lands nothing: a bite being wound up is cancelled, an ability winding up or under way is cancelled, a boss's wind-up and what it has in the air go, and both the Queen's projectile timers reset. Damage does not wake a sleeper (provisional: your followers must be able to hold a sleeping queen; the archive leaves "resistance" open).

**The economy**: Side Quest sells the original for **◆100**; once a potion has put a boss to sleep (the Queen) the next one is **◆50** (`discount`, spent by that purchase); otherwise a replacement is **◆100** (the archive's "lost potion": the game tracks it, he need not see it). The archive's older **"50 coins after a use in combat" is not built**: it conflicts with the 50-shard rule and the game has no coins (docs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md). One original at a time (its stack). The Experiment Lab **copies** the original at the analysis bench for **◆100** (`potionCopyUse`: the original stays; two copies at most).

**Saved**: `potion` (which is in hand, the counts, the discount owed) and `sidequest` (that he appeared, the talk, the promise, the call); 33 fields in all.

## What a player does, in order

1. Go down the culvert behind the arena's north tank. The reading comes as you arrive (your note in the dialogue box): PURIFY THE SEWERS 1/5 in the QUESTS box.
2. Kill the toxic ants (three samples; ten kills in all before the drone comes) and get the water sample from the collapsed tunnel's broken-off floor, any one way: a flux technician's Telegrab from the ledge; an electric technician's launcher by the mouth, which you ride over (tap its tag twice), take the vial, and jump back down from the broken edge; or a fire technician drilling the choke through from the ledge, and you walk in.
3. Take the four to the analysis bench, the cabinet at the west end of the Experiment Lab: ANALYSE, then BUY THE PURIFICATION MODULE ◆300.
4. Back down: once the samples are in and ten ants are dead, the side passage off the south ledge is open and Side Quest calls you over: talk, promise or not, buy the SLEEPING POTION ◆100 and EQUIP it in the pack.
5. The boss gate at the east end of the south ledge opens for the module. The queen sleeps beside the console.
6. INSTALL THE MODULE at the console: she wakes, rooted, throwing toxic bombs and calling her brood; the install holds while she sits there. Sleep her (the POTION pill, or T), or have your squad stun, freeze or glitch her, and the install runs; at half her health she goes frantic and roams: lure her off, sleep her, install. Her bites do not stop the install; walking away does. Or kill her.
7. The module runs thirty seconds; the river clears as it goes. At the end the water is clean, the brood thins to a straggler, and a living queen settles by the console, calm: the quest line is done either way. Side Quest says "…".
