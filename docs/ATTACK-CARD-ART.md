## 1. Intent
The owner, 2026-11-17: "we're about to have to create a whole bunch of different cards that represent attacks. So I wanted to go ahead and create an agent that could create some cool looking attack cards that would involve dynamic shots of elementals using their attacks where they look elementally charged, kind of, and I want the horizon to be slanted, like the camera angle's kind of slanted, and I also want like shocks or fire in the front of the card as focus. And then for like the brawlers, you know, they would have like impact looking cards that have shattered screens and maybe make them look roughed up."

## 2. The brief (the owner's)
- **Elemental cards:** a dynamic shot of the follower using its attack; the follower **elementally charged**; **the horizon slanted** (a tilted camera); **shocks or fire in the front of the card as the focus**.
- **Brawler cards:** **impact**: a **shattered screen**, the card **roughed up**.
- **Technician cards** (the owner, 2026-11-17): "the technician cards are gonna be like surrounded by a very intact ring of crystals while they raise their claws or whatever they have": the follower **inside an unbroken ring of crystals**, **raising its claws** (or whatever limbs it has). The ring is whole and clean, the opposite of a Brawler's shattered card.

## 2b. Basic attack cards (the owner, 2026-11-17)
"Now do a different card for followers' basic attacks. You can use basically generic looking cards for this, but make them have a little bit of character for the different types of attacks that we have in the game."
- **One generic card per attack type**, not per follower: no follower's model on them.
- **The types:** the five the followers' basic attacks use (js/damage.js `DMG_FORM` `main`): **Slashing** (Frost Maw, Electro Skink, Seeper, Rift Mimic), **Piercing** (Quillstone, Hammer Slug, Thermite Burrower, Blight Spider, Sundew), **Crushing** (Static Orb, Goo Proxy, Hover Shell, Igloo Skater, Jelly Ram, Stonepetal), **Projectile** (Rimestar, Hornshell, Rotshard) and **Elemental** (Thunderhead, Voidstar, Ember Hood, Thornwhip). Earthquake is not a follower's basic attack (only the Kinetic Launcher's throw), so it has no card; the agent's reading, provisional.
- **Generic, with a little character:** each card shows its type's kind of hit, simpler and calmer than the follower attack cards, one family.
- **Superseded in part (the owner, 2026-11-17, §4):** the first set (one generic card per type) was rejected; the basic cards are now **each attack type in each element**, made as one attack-type design combined with the element's existing energy-card world and colour. Round 2 shows three designs of Slashing for the owner to choose from (§4).

## 3. Fixed and open
**Fixed (the agent's reading of the brief and the project's rules, not new design):**
- The follower in a card is its own 3-D model (`design/evolutions/`), posed mid-attack, never redrawn.
- The picture fills the card's art area, 225 × 315 CSS px (5:7), baked at 2× (450 × 630); the name plate over its bottom must read.
- Element colours as the game's (`js/config.js` `ELEMENTS`).
- No card's name, stats or effect is invented for a picture.

**Open, for the owner:**
- **Which cards, and how many:** roadmap §12.5 keeps the count and the elements open (six cards, or two per element, were both mentioned). Not to be assumed.
