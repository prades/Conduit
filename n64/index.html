What this is. A working NEC VR4300 (MIPS R4300i) interpreter with the N64 memory map, PI DMA, a disassembler and a stepping debugger. Load a cartridge dump and it boots the way real hardware does — the first 4 KB of the ROM is copied into SP DMEM and execution starts at 0xA4000040, so you are stepping through the cartridge's own IPL3 boot code.
What it is not. This does not play games. That needs the RSP (a second CPU with a 128-bit vector unit), the RDP rasterizer, and high-level emulation of per-game graphics microcode — tens of thousands of lines beyond this. The FPU (COP1), TLB and interrupts are also stubbed. No ROMs are included; load one you dumped yourself.
