# Card battle screen, round 1 (mockups, 2026-11-17)

Pictures of the turn-based card battle screen (`docs/CARD-BATTLES.md`), for the owner to choose a direction. **Proposed, nothing chosen, nothing in the game.** Each is the same moment over a render of the Crystal Chamber in CRYSTAL LAB 3D (round 2's direction 1, from `design/style-evolution/round2`): the squad (Jelly Ram, Stonepetal, Rimestar and the player's cube) against three ants, the player's turn. `N-main.jpg` is the turn; `N-pick.jpg` a card picked and a target chosen. `src/` holds each mockup's page (it reads its pictures from the scratchpad it was made in, so it does not render from here).

1. **Hand of cards**: a fanned hand along the bottom; ready cards up, recharging ones down with their turns.
2. **Squad rows**: one column per follower, its cards under its portrait; the basic attack the squad's own card.
3. **Turn track**: the round's order across the top (you, then each ant with its intent); the cards in a straight rail.
4. **Slab cards**: the game's own upright slab cards (the fight panel's style); the ants as small cards top right.
5. **Cards in the scene**: small fans of cards floating beside each follower; the picked one comes to the centre.
6. **Tactical console**: the home page's instrument look: cartridge cards with charge meters, a battle log, the enemies' next moves.

Every effect line, number, ant move name (but MANDIBLE FRENZY, the striker's own) and the basic attack's rule are the mockup makers', not proposals. TENTACLE LASH is the name of the Jelly Ram's everyday hit's animation (its INFO card says PLAIN HIT).
