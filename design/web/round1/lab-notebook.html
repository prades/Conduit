Now building

Conduit

C.O.R.E. Strategy · Combat Operations Roguelite Evolutions

A top-down survival game in a ruined research facility: restore its power room by room, recruit and evolve a squad of followers, and fight what lives in the arena. Pure canvas, zero dependencies.

The Game Bible holds the whole game on one page: the facility room by room, the classes, every follower form and creature, the rules and their numbers, the interface, how it is built, and what is still to be decided.

On the side

ROM Lab

A sample workstation. Scans a ROM for its instrument samples, or splits an audio file into drum, bass, vocal and instrumental stems, then plays them on pads with a step sequencer, FX and MP3 export.
